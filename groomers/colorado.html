Before you book a Poodle

  • Ask whether they clip Poodles regularly and which clips they offer (sporting, lamb, teddy).
  • Say how matted the coat is. A matted coat may need to be clipped short; the matting hotspots map shows what to check first.
  • Ask how often they suggest coming back. The AKC says many Poodle owners book every four to six weeks.
  • For a puppy, ask for a short, gentle first visit. See the coat change timeline for when the hard months start.

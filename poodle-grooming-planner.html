The printable PDF: what is inside

  1. Your grooming year. A month-by-month table for groomer visits, home face and feet tidies and nails. The AKC says many Poodle owners book a groomer every four to six weeks.
  2. Matting hotspot map. Eight numbered zones on a body outline, with a Monday, Wednesday and Saturday check. The zones follow Preventive Vet's list of friction spots.
  3. Puppy coat change tracker. Months 6 to 16, with what you see at the roots and whether mats are back. Standards usually change later than Toys and Minis.
  4. Daily and weekly routine. A three-minute daily comb, after-walk checks and bath-day steps you can tick off.
  5. Home kit checklist. Brush, metal comb, detangling spray, dryer, clipper and nail tool, with what each one is for.

How to use it

Print pages 3 and 5 and keep them where you groom. Fill in page 2 when you book the next appointment. If your Poodle is under a year old, use page 4 every month so you notice the coat change early, when daily combing matters most.

Each page names its sources. The full guides behind it are here on the site: the matting hotspots map, the puppy coat change timeline, the Bravura2 clipper review and how much exercise a Standard needs.

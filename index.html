04 · How the guides are made

From a forum question to a page you can use

  1. Start from a real question

    Each guide begins with something owners keep asking in Poodle forums and groups.

  2. Find who knows

    Manufacturer specs, breed clubs and working groomers, each linked where it is used.

  3. Make it usable

    A map, checker or planner you can try on your own dog, near the top of the page.

  4. Read it twice

    Drafted with AI help, read by a person before it goes live, corrected when you write in.

Poodle Guide is not a vet clinic or a grooming salon. Guides explain what breed clubs, manufacturers and groomers say, link each source in the text, and point you to a professional when home care is not enough. Read the editorial policy.
